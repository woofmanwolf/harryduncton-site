My independence rules

I'm a working journalist. These are not negotiable, and they are the reason my advice is worth paying for.

  1. I never guarantee coverage, and I don't sell access to editors.
  2. I don't review, rate or recommend a client's products in any publication while we work together, or for 12 months afterwards.
  3. The editors I write for know which brands I consult for.
  4. If I introduce you to anyone in the media, they'll know it's paid work.
  5. If your product isn't ready for UK reviewers, I'll tell you, even if it's not what you want to hear.
